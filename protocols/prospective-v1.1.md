**Question and methods.** Evaluate the original deterministic ETSS full total-water-level forecast at its provider-confirmed valid times against unchanged Slackwater tide, the plain mean of the last 24 hourly observed-minus-Slackwater residuals added to future tide, and persistence of the last hourly residual added to future tide. The existing three-hour detided anchor plus issued SURGE change remains a separate regression comparator, with its code and input hashes frozen before calibration. Full TWL already includes tide and BIAS: never add an observation anchor or BIAS again. This comparison benchmarks the native product; any new smooth-background candidate needs a separate formula/code preregistration before examining its calibration outcomes.

**Stations and targets.** Friday Harbor 9449880/FRDW1, Port Townsend 9444900/PTWW1 and Seattle 9447130/EBSW1. Daily decisions at 02:00 UTC, forecasting +3, +6, +12 and +24 hours, all heights in meters on MLLW. The requested model run is the preceding day's 18Z cycle. Use exact target times; do not tune a clock offset, interpolate around a missing hour, or stitch later runs.

**Dates.** Calibration decisions November 1 through November 30, 2026. Confirmation decisions December 4, 2026 through January 31, 2027. The gap separates calibration targets and the 35 hours of later support used for rapid-event labels from the first confirmation decision. Freeze interval widths and all code/input hashes before fetching or computing confirmation outcomes. If prerequisites are not ready, record this cohort as unavailable or inconclusive; any replacement dates require a new prospective protocol before their outcomes are inspected. Do not extend the cohort because its scores disappoint.

**Availability.** Select the earliest complete, hash-verified paired capture of the requested run received by the decision, with a deterministic manifest-ID tie-breaker. Keep every revision but never select a later revision using its error. Both exports must pass the provider-confirmed mapping and relevant station support contract. Do not substitute an older cycle if the requested run is unavailable. Require all observation inputs to have been received by the decision; the newest allowed sample is 01:00 UTC. For each observation sample, select the latest eligible valid receipt at or before the decision, with lexicographically smallest manifest ID as a tie-breaker, and retain the chosen body hash. Never substitute a revision received later. Preserve preliminary observations and quality flags as received, using a preregistered normalization policy under #396. Later verified observations may supply truth, never decision inputs. Model cycle, bulletin creation, HTTP modification and actual receipt are separate clocks. A manual retrospective download cannot prove decision-time availability.

**Fallback and denominator.** Unavailable, late, ambiguous or malformed forecast/history yields unchanged tide for that method. Evaluate every scheduled station-decision with valid targets, including fallback, and also report the corrected subset. Report missing targets separately by gauge/horizon; do not require all four targets merely to retain one valid horizon. Count acquisition gaps separately from confirmed provider failures. The preservation command alone does not establish continuous collection or observation receipt coverage; those are prerequisites for this prospective trial.

**Point acceptance.** At every gauge/horizon, overall and rapid-case MAE and RMSE must be no worse than unchanged tide. Overall +24-hour RMSE must be strictly lower at every gauge. Report the mean24, persistence and fixed surge-change comparators, including paired differences; beating native tide alone does not establish that a new smoother improves the existing candidate. Any proposed smoother must additionally beat the fixed existing surge-change correction's +24-hour RMSE at each gauge before acceptance.

**Uncertainty.** Calibrate each gauge/horizon's linear empirical 90th absolute-error percentile on all calibration cases, including fallback. Use the maximum of the three gauge radii as the shared radius at each horizon. Require all gauges to contribute calibration and at least 90% confirmation coverage overall and for rapid cases at every gauge/horizon. Freeze widths before confirmation outcomes. Never widen them on confirmation results. Report radius, coverage and counts together.

**Rapid events and sample size.** Label rapid cases only after the fact: absolute change in the centered hourly Godin background from decision to +24 hours at least 0.10 m. Godin requires its complete 71-hour support; absent support means an unknown label. Use all and only valid normalized observations for this diagnostic, and never feed the label into a prediction. Require at least 20 scorable confirmation dates and five rapid dates at each gauge/horizon, spanning at least three episodes; assign episode IDs on the full daily label calendar before filtering for horizon truth. Rapid dates belong to the same episode until at least one known nonrapid date separates them; unknown-label gaps cannot establish separate episodes. Unknown labels break neither the uncertainty disclosure nor the minimum-sample requirement: show their count and do not count them as nonrapid. Insufficient evidence is inconclusive. Nearby cycles, overlapping targets and dates within one storm are dependent, so they do not count as independent storm events.

**Report and gates.** Per gauge/horizon report MAE, RMSE, p95 and maximum absolute error, corrected/fallback counts and reasons, missing truth, unknown rapid labels, interval coverage/radius, worst episodes and leave-one-date-out sensitivity. Preserve all outliers.
